## A different kind of legal AI engineering project

Locke Prime is a firm-scoped legal software platform combining matter workspaces, document intelligence, legal research, contract lifecycle workflows, litigation preparation, collaboration, and an assistant called **Ask Locke**. Built as an integrated Node.js / Express + PostgreSQL application, it demonstrates end-to-end product architecture, live deployment, and sustained security-focused iteration.

The strategic differentiation is in the **workflow composition**: a matter's facts flow into document retrieval, research, adversarial drafting review, trial proof mapping, and carefully controlled next steps.

> **Why this portfolio exists:** For organizations considering Locke Development for software engineering, AI integration, product implementation, and workflow automation — and for employers evaluating the technical depth of delivered applications. This is not a consumer sales funnel.

### What the implementation demonstrates

| Demonstrated capability | Concrete engineering outcome |
|---|---|
| **Ask Locke — attorney-controlled AI assistant** | Matter-aware chat, contextual retrieval, citations, research mode, and validated task/deadline/note proposals that require confirmation before saving. |
| **Boardroom** | A structured legal review with co-counsel, opposing counsel, and judge viewpoints, informed by source authority and firm experience where available. |
| **War Room** | Witness and exhibit records, element-to-proof map, readiness scoring, and attack/gap/synthesis planning scripts. |
| **Document intelligence** | OCR, large-file indexing, source-linked search, local/removable file source support, tracked-changes DOCX output. |
| **Contract intelligence** | Clause playbooks, deviation review, obligation records, approval states, portfolio risk analysis. |
| **Firm-governed operations** | Matter access, ethical walls, action approvals, entitlement enforcement, logging, retention controls, AI usage reporting. |
