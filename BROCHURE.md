# LOCKE PRIME

### An integrated legal AI platform — and evidence of full-stack product engineering

**Portfolio report for future clients, collaborators, and employers** · November 13, 2026

> **The key differentiator is not an AI chatbot. It is a connected record-to-decision workflow.** Locke Prime brings the matter, its documents, available authority, adversarial reasoning, proof readiness, and attorney-approved action into one controlled environment.

## The case for Locke Prime

**1. AI that works with the matter.** Ask Locke uses matter metadata, document retrieval, page-aware citations, and context about the application page. It can propose a task, note, or deadline while requiring an explicit user confirmation before persistence.

**2. Explicitly adversarial legal review.** The Boardroom offers judge, co-counsel and opposing-counsel perspectives plus structured draft review. Judge context can use retrieved authority and retained firm-specific experience — the latter is treated as experience, not law.

**3. Evidence before advocacy.** The War Room maps witnesses, exhibits, tasks, and matter records into a proof-readiness view, then supports attack, gap, and synthesis scripts tied to the record.

**4. Documents you can interrogate and improve.** OCR, hybrid retrieval, long-document indexing, DOCX tracked changes, contract clause/playbook analysis and firm-directed retention control connect operational and substantive workflows.

**5. Operational controls, not just prototypes.** PostgreSQL-backed matter isolation, ethical-wall restrictions, bounded inputs, credential handling, audit trails, and remediated security weaknesses exist in the source; those features have configuration and independent-verification boundaries described in the audit.

### Why these workflows are a stronger portfolio story

A buyer or hiring manager can see more than an LLM API call: the project spans information ingestion, matter-scoped authorization, legal-source retrieval, grounded generation, structured validation, review-and-confirm UX, document formats, and production deployment.

**Where competing platforms lead:** Clio Work offers extensive legal-library and drafting integrations; Filevine LOIS offers firm-wide agentic workflow automation and live deposition intelligence; Litify ACE offers automated case workflows and Salesforce extensibility; MyCase and Smokeball offer mature client-facing practice management, billing and financial operations. Locke's documented strengths are narrower and more architecturally specific.

### A valuable distinction, carefully stated

The combination of a structured three-seat Boardroom, proof-first War Room, firm-specific court/judge learning, and assistant-confirmed operational actions is **differentiated in the reviewed source and publicly visible vendor descriptions**. It is **not independently proven to outperform** every competitor on speed, legal accuracy, scale, security, or outcomes.
